Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-14

Leonida Log does not collect, store, or transmit personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics.

What the app stores

Your progress (which items you have marked collected, your settings, and your dashboard layout) is saved on your device only, in the app’s local storage and a shared container that the app’s widgets read. It is not sent anywhere and we cannot see it, unless you switch on the stream overlay (below). Deleting the app removes it.

Stream overlay (optional)

If you switch on More › Stream overlay, the app sends your progress figures and the names of the last few things you ticked off to our overlay server, so your stream can show them. Anyone with your overlay link can see those figures. If you type a channel name, it is sent too, and is used only to label how many viewers scanned the QR code on your overlay; it is never shown on the overlay. Scans are counted per day and nothing is recorded about the viewer who scanned. Switching the overlay off removes your figures from the server straight away. See the streamer guide.

Purchases

Optional Premium features are sold through Apple’s in-app purchase system. Apple processes the payment and the subscription; the app only receives a yes or no entitlement status from Apple and never sees your payment details. Apple’s handling of that transaction is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Content loaded from the internet

The app downloads its location data, reference images and announcements from our content host, reads public news feeds from games websites (such as IGN, GameSpot and Eurogamer) to show GTA VI stories, and opens video walkthroughs on YouTube in your browser or the YouTube app. Opening a news story takes you to that website, whose own privacy policy applies. Making those requests reveals your device’s IP address to the host serving the file, as with any web request; we do not log it or use it to identify you. Once you open a YouTube link, YouTube’s own privacy policy applies.

Reporting an issue

If you report a problem from inside the app, it opens your email app with a pre-filled message that includes the app version, your device model, and any details you added, such as a corrected map location. Nothing is sent until you choose to send it.

Children

Leonida Log is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
