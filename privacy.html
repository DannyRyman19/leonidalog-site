Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-20

Leonida Log has no account, no sign-in and no analytics, and we don’t collect personal information ourselves. The free version shows ads, and the ad partners that serve them collect some data from your device, as set out under Advertising. Premium has no ads.

What the app stores

Your progress (which items you have marked collected, your settings, and your dashboard layout) is saved on your device only, in the app’s local storage and a shared container that the app’s widgets read. It is not sent to us and we cannot see it, unless you switch on the stream overlay (below). Deleting the app removes it.

With Premium’s iCloud Sync on, the same progress, playthroughs and notes are also kept in your own iCloud account, so they match on your iPhone and iPad. That copy is stored by Apple under your Apple Account; we cannot see it. You can switch iCloud Sync off in the app, or stop it for Leonida Log in your device’s iCloud settings.

Stream overlay (optional)

If you switch on More › Stream overlay, the app sends your progress figures and the names of the last few things you ticked off to our overlay server, so your stream can show them. Anyone with your overlay link can see those figures. If you type a channel name, it is sent too, and is used only to label how many viewers scanned the QR code on your overlay; it is never shown on the overlay. Scans are counted per day and nothing is recorded about the viewer who scanned. Switching the overlay off removes your figures from the server straight away. See the streamer guide.

Purchases

Optional Premium features are sold through Apple’s in-app purchase system. Apple processes the payment and the subscription; the app only receives a yes or no entitlement status from Apple and never sees your payment details. Apple’s handling of that transaction is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Advertising

The free version shows ads through Unity LevelPlay, which serves them from Unity Ads. There are two kinds:

  • an ad you choose to watch when you reach the free tracking limit, which lets you track 10 more items;
  • now and then a short full-screen ad after you finish a set (at most one every 10 minutes, and none in your first two days with the app).

To show and measure these ads, Unity and its ad partners may collect your device’s identifiers (the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking), your IP address and the rough location it gives, your device model, language and connection type, and how you interact with ads. We don’t receive this data. Unity’s use of it is covered by Unity’s privacy policy, where you can also ask Unity to delete data linked to your device.

If you live in the EU, the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app first asks whether ad partners may use this data to personalise ads. Saying no still shows ads, just not personalised ones, and you can change your answer at any time under More › Personalised ads.

The app then asks, through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency, whether it may track you. If you don’t allow it, your advertising identifier isn’t shared. You can change that answer at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Nothing is asked and the ad services aren’t started before your second time in the app, unless you choose to watch an ad sooner. With Premium the app never starts them.

Content loaded from the internet

The app downloads its location data, reference images and announcements from our content host, reads public news feeds from games websites (such as IGN, GameSpot and Eurogamer) to show GTA VI stories, and opens video walkthroughs on YouTube in your browser or the YouTube app. Opening a news story takes you to that website, whose own privacy policy applies. Making those requests reveals your device’s IP address to the host serving the file, as with any web request; we do not log it or use it to identify you. Once you open a YouTube link, YouTube’s own privacy policy applies.

Reporting an issue

If you report a problem from inside the app, it opens your email app with a pre-filled message that includes the app version, your device model, and any details you added, such as a corrected map location. Nothing is sent until you choose to send it.

Children

Leonida Log is not directed at children, and we don’t knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
