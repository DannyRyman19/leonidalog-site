For streamers

Put your 100% run on stream

Leonida Log can show your progress live on Twitch, YouTube or Kick. Tick something off in the app and your ring updates on stream within seconds, with the item you just found. It’s free and works in OBS, Streamlabs and anything else that takes a browser source.

It’s early days. As more checklists and features arrive in Leonida Log, the overlay will get more to show and more ways to make it your own. Check back here for updates.

Set it up

  1. In Leonida Log, open More › Stream overlay and switch on Show my progress on stream.
  2. Tap Copy link and send it to the computer you stream from (AirDrop, Notes or a message to yourself).
  3. In OBS, add a Browser source and paste the link as its URL. In Streamlabs, add a Browser Source widget. Set the width and height for your layout (below).
  4. Drag it into a corner over your gameplay. The background is transparent.

Keep Leonida Log open on your phone while you play. The overlay updates from the app, so a tick shows on stream as soon as the app sends it.

Pick a layout

Add ?layout= to the end of your link to choose one. Leave it off for the card. These previews run on made-up figures.

Card

The default: ring, count and a QR code to the app. Source size 760 × 300.

Vertical

A tall column for the side of the screen or portrait streams. Add ?layout=vertical. Source size 300 × 600.

Minimal

Just the ring and the count, for a busy HUD. Add ?layout=minimal. Source size 480 × 200.

Want to try one in OBS first? Use https://leonidalog.rymandanny.workers.dev/overlay/demo (with any ?layout=) as the URL. It shows the same made-up run.

More options

Join options with &, for example ?layout=minimal&scale=1.5.

OptionWhat it does
scale=1.5Makes everything bigger (or smaller, below 1). Handy on a 1440p or 4K canvas. Make the source size bigger by the same amount.
recent=0Never shows what you just ticked off. You can also switch this off in the app with Show what I just ticked off.
metric=hundredPlusShows 100%+ instead of the figure chosen in the app. metric=pathTo100 does the reverse.

Something not right?

The overlay is blank. Check that Show my progress on stream is on in the app, that you copied the current link, and that Leonida Log has been opened since you switched it on.

It isn’t updating. Open Leonida Log on your phone. The overlay checks for new figures every 10 seconds, but the app has to be open to send them.

It shows the wrong run, or someone else has your link. In the app, tap Get a new link. The old one stops working straight away, so paste the new one into OBS.

It’s cut off. Make the browser source bigger, using the sizes above (times your scale).

What’s shared

While the overlay is on, your progress figures and the names of the last few things you ticked off are sent to Leonida Log’s server, and anyone with your link can see them. Nothing else is. Switch it off in the app and the overlay goes blank. See the privacy policy.
