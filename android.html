Leonida Log for Android

Help test it

Leonida Log is coming to Google Play. Before it can launch, Google asks for a group of testers to have it installed for two weeks. It’s free, it’s the full app, and your progress carries over to the finished version.

Do both steps on your Android phone or tablet, signed in to the Google account you use for the Play Store.

  1. 1. Join the testers group

    This is how Google knows to let you in. Open the group and tap Join group.

    Join the group

  2. 2. Get the app

    Tap Become a tester, then install Leonida Log from Google Play as usual.

    Become a tester

Good to know

If step 2 says the app isn’t available, step 1 hasn’t gone through yet: check you joined the group with the same Google account, wait a minute and try again.

Please keep the app installed for at least 14 days; that’s the part Google counts.

Anything broken or wrong? Tap the flag on any item, use More › Send general feedback, or email support@leonidalog.app.

On iPhone or iPad? Leonida Log is on the App Store.
